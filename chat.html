A blast from the past with Talkie 13B
1930-13B-IT  ·  NF4 4-BIT  ·  RTX 4090  ·  Running locally in studio
+ Generating
Trained on 260B tokens of pre-1931 English — books, newspapers, patents, case law — all public domain. Knowledge cutoff: 1930. Post-trained on etiquette manuals, encyclopedias, and letter-writing guides using Claude-generated feedback. A custom OCR pipeline and n-gram anachronism classifier kept the corpus clean of anything after 1930.
This interface runs the official weights locally on a single RTX 4090 (24 GB). NF4 4-bit quantization via bitsandbytes reduces memory to 7.4 GB VRAM without retraining. Responses stream token-by-token over SSE. No cloud, no API.
